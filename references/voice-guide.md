# Voice guide for clearly labeled simulations

These are writing cues inferred from the conversation, not scripts or claims about how either person would actually answer a new question. Read topic-specific evidence before using them. A simulated response must be labeled **模拟回答** outside the first-person text.

## Alan / 邵艾伦

- Often opens with a concrete person or stage of life, then turns the issue into a pointed question. He tests the practical consequence of Sun's framework and restates it in accessible terms. See his own ten-year wealth strategy and follow-up question at [01:21:58, transcript line 779](transcript.md#L779), and his “认知上的提升” restatement at [02:45:29, line 1621](transcript.md#L1621).
- Uses game-like framing to explore what comes after an achievement: wealth as a “门票”, self-chosen side quests, and no single correct path. See [03:55:39, line 2183](transcript.md#L2183) and [03:57:36, lines 2201–2203](transcript.md#L2201).
- Can voice a tentative synthesis, challenge an easy slogan, and ask for executable steps. Do not turn his interview questions into firm beliefs unless he explicitly endorses them. Avoid empty interviewer filler and exaggerated catchphrases.

- Frequently begins with “我觉得”, proposes a principle, then expands it through an analogy, personal example, or feedback loop. His “先自由后财富” explanation connects cognition, flexibility, opportunity, and wealth. See [02:43:27, lines 1611–1617](transcript.md#L1611) and [02:45:32, lines 1623–1629](transcript.md#L1623).
- Connects domains: AI tools and documentation, health, learning, and work are often part of one personal system. See [00:07:07, line 105](transcript.md#L105), [02:40:59, line 1597](transcript.md#L1597), and [02:53:53, line 1713](transcript.md#L1713).
- On life after wealth, he values new perspectives and experience; on activity, he favors creation with consumption as a complement. See [03:52:24, lines 2167–2171](transcript.md#L2167) and [04:15:46, line 2331](transcript.md#L2331).
- Keep the reasoning energetic and concrete, but avoid making his speculative claims sound like verified facts or reproducing excessive verbal tics.

## Extrapolating into a new situation

Infer a plausible line of reasoning from multiple relevant passages, then write a fresh answer to the user's circumstances. Do not reverse a recorded position to make the simulation more dramatic; put any contrasting idea in your own analysis instead. A topic with no direct discussion needs an explicit “无直接原文” note in the evidence section. Never fabricate dialogue between the two, exact quotes, future actions, endorsements, or personal details.
